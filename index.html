Core Technical Security Disciplines & Assessment Services

We provide deep-dive technical offensive assessments and defensive engineering audits tailored for high-assurance organizations. Explore our core service capabilities:

Digital Forensics & Evidence Reconstruction

When a security incident, breach, or insider threat occurs, Defensive Cyber uncovers, preserves, and analyzes digital evidence across endpoints, volatile memory, cloud estates, and networks. Following strict chain-of-custody protocols, our certified senior practitioners reconstruct the complete chronological timeline of adversary activity to deliver defensible, court-admissible forensic reports that hold up under legal, regulatory, and executive scrutiny.

Key specialties include volatile RAM triage, disk imaging, cloud audit log forensics, and anti-forensics evasion detection.

Cyber Threat Intelligence & Adversary Tracking

Defensive Cyber proactively monitors adversary infrastructure, dark web forums, illicit marketplaces, and leaked credentials to identify threats targeting your organization before attacks occur. We translate real-world threat actor behaviors and campaigns into actionable intelligence, prioritized threat warnings, and tailored detection rules to harden your defenses in advance.

Emergency Incident Response & Breach Containment

In the event of an active intrusion, compromised identity, or ransomware deployment, Defensive Cyber delivers rapid, senior-led emergency containment to sever adversary command links, halt lateral spread, and preserve vital evidence. We systematically eliminate backdoors and persistence mechanisms, orchestrating a safe, resilient operational recovery with minimal business downtime.

Our emergency response SLA ensures 24-hour rapid mobilization, forensic evidence preservation, and structured post-incident remediation roadmaps.

Reverse Engineering & Malware Disassembly

Defensive Cyber dissects suspicious binaries, compiled executables, loaders, and obfuscated payloads down to the assembly level inside isolated laboratory environments. We expose hidden capabilities, encryption routines, and command-and-control infrastructure, providing validated detection signatures (YARA and Sigma) and tactical indicators to permanently block related threat variants.

Our malware analysis team delivers static disassembly, dynamic behavioral sandboxing, memory unpackers, and weaponized payload deobfuscation.
